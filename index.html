Privacy Policy for Ati Campus

App Name: Ati Campus  |  Last updated: November 26, 2026

Ati Campus is an educational and student resource application designed to help students access study notes, past papers, and academic materials, and to participate in group discussions.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in to or use Ati Campus, the following information may be collected and processed:

  • Account & Profile Information: Basic details such as your name, email address, and student registration details when signing in via Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication.
  • Educational & App Content: Study notes, learning resources, and messages posted within the group chat.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: Basic app performance, connectivity, or error logs needed to keep the service running reliably.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information strictly to:

  • Authenticate student accounts and manage access to app features.
  • Deliver and load study notes and educational content through Firebase services.
  • Support group chat communication and maintain safety/moderation within the group.
  • Troubleshoot issues, prevent misuse, and improve app stability.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal user data to third parties. Ati Campus relies on Google Firebase services (such as authentication, database, and storage) to host and deliver core app functionality. Data processed through these services is subject to Google's privacy terms and security practices.

4. Data Retention and Account / Data Deletion

We retain user account and app data only for as long as needed to provide Ati Campus educational and group features or where retention is required for safety, abuse prevention, or legal obligations.

How to Request Account and Data Deletion for Ati Campus

If you are an Ati Campus user and want your account and associated personal data deleted, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to chalanamadhawa63@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Ati Campus account (or clearly state your registered email and student registration details).
  2. Use the subject line: "Account and Data Deletion Request - Ati Campus".
  3. Once verified, we will process your deletion request within a reasonable timeframe (typically within 7 to 14 days).

What is deleted: Associated user profile/account records and personal user data linked to the account across app storage/database services, subject to technical system removal cycles.

What may be retained: Limited security, moderation, audit, or legal compliance logs if required by law or platform safety rules, retained only for as long as necessary for those purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

Ati Campus is intended for higher-education/campus students and general users and does not knowingly collect personal information from young children without appropriate permission.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy and account deletion information from time to time as the app evolves. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
