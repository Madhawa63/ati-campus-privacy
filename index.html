Privacy Policy for Ati Campus

Last updated: November 2026

Ati Campus is an educational and student resource application designed to help students access study notes, past papers, and participate in group discussions.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in or use the app, we may collect basic account and profile information (such as your name, email address, and student registration details via Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication), study materials or notes data, and messages sent within the group chat.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to authenticate users, deliver study resources and notes via Firebase, enable group chat functionality, maintain app security, and improve the user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Firebase services (such as authentication, database, and storage) to operate core features. Data processed through these services is subject to Google's privacy practices.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain user data only as long as necessary to provide app services. Users or administrators can request account or data removal by contacting us.
